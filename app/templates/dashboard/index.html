How these figures are calculated
Scope
{{ scope.label }}. Administrators, auditors and evidence custodians see the whole lab; investigators see the cases they are assigned to. Every figure is one SQL query run when the page loads.
Current state
Figures at this moment; the period selector doesn't change them.
Open cases
Status is Open, In Progress or On Hold (anything except Closed).
High-priority open
Open cases whose priority is Critical or High.
Awaiting examination
Evidence linked to at least one examination that is still Pending (not yet started).
Under examination
Evidence whose current custody status is Under Examination.
Pending transfers
Checked Out or In Transit: out of storage and still to be returned or received.
Completed examinations
All examinations with status Completed.
Reports pending review
Reports with status Under Review, waiting for an administrator who isn't the author.
Integrity
Latest check of each item's current reference hash: Verified; Integrity mismatch (the latest check failed); Pending verification (hash recorded, never checked); Verification unavailable (no hash recorded). A matching hash shows the content was identical when compared; it doesn't prove authenticity on its own.
Activity in the period
Records created in the selected period: cases by registration time, evidence by registration time, custody entries by the time the handover happened, hash checks by check time, examinations by completion time.
Needs attention
Examinations past their due date that are still Pending or In Progress; items with an integrity mismatch; reports Under Review{% if show_requests %}; pending account requests{% endif %}.
