Why FORGE-X?

Many small labs still track evidence in spreadsheets and paper forms. These are the gaps that causes, and how FORGE-X closes each one.

The usual gapIn FORGE-X
Custody forms go missing or get filled in after the fact.Each handover is saved in the same transaction that updates the evidence item.
Hash values sit in notes and filenames.Original hashes are stored once, and every later check is kept, failures included.
Anyone with the spreadsheet can change anything.What you can do depends on your role, checked on the server for every request.
Edits leave no trace.Custody entries and audit logs can't be edited. Mistakes are fixed with a linked correction.

About the project

FORGE-X is a B.Tech Computer Science project for a database management systems course. It shows how a relational database can support a forensic laboratory's record keeping: cases, evidence, custody, examinations and reports.

It is a learning project, not a certified forensic product. All records in the demo are synthetic, and the system records examination activity rather than running forensic tools on devices.
